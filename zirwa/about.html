About Zirwa

Clothing that feels like you

Zirwa Clothing Store is a women's clothing store on Lyallpur Commercial Road in Faisalabad, a city known across Pakistan for its fabric and tailoring.

We started with a simple idea: elegant, modern clothes should not feel out of reach. Our collection covers dresses, co-ords, tops, bottoms and abayas, with a few unisex pieces for the whole family.

You can visit the shop at any hour, since we are open 24 hours, or order online and message us on WhatsApp. We answer questions about sizes, colours and delivery personally.
