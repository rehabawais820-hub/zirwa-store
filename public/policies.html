Store policies

Shipping

We deliver across Pakistan. Shipping is free on orders above PKR 4,000. Below that, a flat fee of PKR 250 applies.

After you place your order on WhatsApp we confirm it with you, then dispatch. We share the delivery time for your city when we confirm.

Returns and exchanges

If your size does not fit or the item arrives damaged, message us on WhatsApp as soon as you receive it, with a photo. We will arrange an exchange where stock allows.

Items should be unworn, unwashed and in their original condition.

Privacy policy

We collect only the details you give us for an order: name, phone number, city and delivery address. We use them to confirm and deliver your order and to answer your questions.

Your cart and saved details stay on your own device. We do not sell your information.

Terms and conditions

Prices are in Pakistani rupees (PKR). Colours may look slightly different on your screen. An order is confirmed once we message you back on WhatsApp.

We may update prices and stock without notice. For any question, call or WhatsApp +92 331 7756688.
